# Landing `/` (src/pages/Landing.tsx)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: owner of a 5–100 car rental company renting to app drivers, running on spreadsheets and WhatsApp. Action: choose a management plan (`/checkout/gestao-basico|pro|master`); secondary: Entrar (`/login`, or `/dashboard` with session). Proof: the product's own operation shown as labeled example data; no invented numbers, testimonials, or annual pricing. Constraints: plans differ only by limits (vehicles, drivers, users).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The rental company as its key board ("quadro de chaves"): every hook is a car, every tag's color says where it is. Refuses the split hero with dashboard screenshot, feature-card grid, and three floating pricing cards.

OWN-WORLD: Natural Eucatex hardboard (#B07A45) with a real perforation grid as the layout grid; plastic key tags in status colors (azul alugado, verde disponível, amarelo oficina, vermelho atrasado) with paper inserts showing Mercosul-style plates; embossed label-maker strips for headings and nav; graphite steel (#24282C) for the cabinet sections. Archivo (expanded black) for display, Saira Condensed for plates and numbers.

STORY: Visitor sees their own board, understands one tag holds the whole car record (driver, weekly charge, inspection, workshop, insurance), sees the 7-step inspection, picks the plan by how many hooks they need.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Board fills the viewport. Left 5 cols: label-strip nav on the top rail, headline set large on the board, one line of sub, "Escolher meu plano" primary + Entrar. Right 7 cols: 4×4 hooks of example tags, legend strip, "exemplo" label.

FORM: Quadro de Chaves, my rank 1 (IMPECCABLE'S PICK), seed key d5c64adf. Signature interaction: tags hang and swing from their hook; clicking a tag cycles its status in place; fleet-size picker in pricing lights the plan tag that fits.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
